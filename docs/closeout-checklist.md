# Project Closeout Checklist

## Technical and quality

- [ ] Approved as-built drawings delivered
- [ ] Final relay settings and coordination records delivered
- [ ] FAT and SAT reports approved
- [ ] All Category A and B punch items closed with evidence
- [ ] Equipment manuals, spare-parts list, and warranties delivered
- [ ] Nonconformance and defect records closed

## Customer

- [ ] Customer training completed and attendance recorded
- [ ] Open actions and decisions reviewed
- [ ] Customer acceptance certificate signed
- [ ] Customer Care handoff completed
- [ ] Satisfaction and lessons-learned discussion completed

## Commercial and financial

- [ ] Approved changes incorporated into contract value and schedule
- [ ] Supplier invoices, commitments, accruals, and credits reconciled
- [ ] Final customer invoice issued
- [ ] Forecast at completion reconciled to actual cost
- [ ] Contingency balance released
- [ ] Financial closeout approved

## Administrative

- [ ] Final status report distributed
- [ ] Project records indexed and archived
- [ ] RAID and change logs closed
- [ ] Lessons learned captured with future actions
- [ ] Team and vendor performance feedback recorded
