# Commissioning and Customer Acceptance Plan

## Safety boundary

This portfolio project is a project-control simulation, not an operating procedure. Actual medium-voltage work must be performed by qualified personnel under the owner’s safety program, approved switching orders, applicable codes, and manufacturer instructions.

## Factory acceptance test gate

- Approved drawings and test procedure available
- Nameplate, dimensions, bus, wiring, control power, and interlocks inspected
- Breaker mechanical/electrical operation and control logic tested
- Relay configuration records captured
- Metering and communication checks completed
- Findings categorized, assigned, and closed or formally accepted
- Customer and quality signatures captured before shipment release

## Site acceptance and energization gate

- Equipment receiving inspection complete
- Installation torque and connection records available
- Grounding, control wiring, and point-to-point checks complete
- Insulation and functional test records approved
- Final protection settings installed and independently verified
- Control, interlock, trip, close, and communication functions demonstrated
- Category A punch-list items closed
- Customer operating personnel trained
- Energization authorization signed by responsible parties

## Punch-list classification

- **Category A:** safety, protection, operability, or energization blocker; must close before energization
- **Category B:** non-blocking documentation, labeling, finish, or minor functional item; dated closure plan required
- **Category C:** enhancement or preference outside acceptance criteria; route through change control

## Acceptance evidence

Approved as-builts, final settings, FAT/SAT reports, equipment manuals, warranty records, training attendance, punch-list closure evidence, signed acceptance certificate, and approved financial reconciliation.
