# Customer Communication Plan

## Objectives

- Maintain one source of truth for scope, schedule, financial changes, risks, actions, and acceptance evidence.
- Provide early warning and decision-ready options instead of reporting problems after milestones are missed.
- Record commitments, owners, and dates after every customer or cross-functional meeting.

## Cadence

| Communication | Audience | Cadence | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Internal execution stand-up | PM, engineering, factory, field service | Weekly | PM | Actions, blockers, critical path |
| Customer progress meeting | Customer, PM, engineering, Customer Care | Biweekly; weekly during site work | PM | Status report and minutes |
| Financial review | PM, finance, operations | Monthly | PM | Baseline, actual, forecast, variance |
| RAID escalation | Sponsor and impacted leads | Within 24 hours for red items | PM | Impact, options, recommendation |
| FAT readiness review | Customer, factory, quality, PM | 10 and 2 days before FAT | Factory lead | Readiness checklist |
| Commissioning coordination | Customer, field service, PM | Daily during SAT | Field lead | Test status and punch list |
| Closeout review | Customer, PM, Customer Care, finance | Weekly until acceptance | PM | Deliverable and financial status |

## Status-report structure

1. Overall health and executive summary
2. Accomplishments since last report
3. Next milestones and customer inputs required
4. Schedule and critical-path changes
5. Financial baseline, forecast, and approved changes
6. Top risks, issues, decisions, and escalations
7. FAT/SAT, punch-list, and acceptance readiness
8. Action log with owner and due date

## Meeting-minutes standard

Minutes are distributed within one business day and include date, attendees, purpose, decisions, action items, owners, due dates, open questions, and attachments. Unchallenged minutes become the shared record after two business days.
