# Project Charter — MV-24

## Project

North Carolina Municipal Substation Expansion — Medium-Voltage Switchgear and Commissioning

## Business need

The municipal utility requires added feeder capacity, improved protection coordination, and a maintainable 15 kV distribution lineup before the summer reliability window. The project combines customer requirements, engineered equipment, factory production, site services, testing, and controlled energization.

## Objectives and success measures

1. Deliver the approved 15 kV equipment scope and complete energization by 8 August.
2. Maintain forecast at completion within the $2.84M internal baseline.
3. Close all Category A punch-list items before energization.
4. Achieve customer acceptance with all eight required closeout deliverables.
5. Maintain zero recordable safety incidents and 100% mandatory test-record completion.

## In scope

- Customer requirements review and approved submittals
- 15 kV metal-clad switchgear lineup, main breaker, four feeder breakers, relays, metering, and controls
- Factory engineering, manufacturing coordination, FAT, shipment, receiving inspection, field installation support, SAT, energization, and customer training
- Project schedule, financial tracking, RAID, change control, status reporting, documentation, acceptance, and closeout

## Out of scope

- Utility upstream protection redesign
- Civil building construction
- Customer-owned medium-voltage cable installation
- Permanent operations staffing after training and acceptance

## Constraints and assumptions

- Energization must occur inside the approved outage window.
- Customer-furnished cable schedules and relay settings are required inputs.
- Field access is limited to the site safety plan and permit window.
- Values, dates, stakeholders, and equipment records are synthetic for educational use.

## Governance

- Executive sponsor: Business Unit Operations Director
- Project manager: Accountable for scope, schedule, financials, risk, customer communication, and closeout
- Engineering lead: Design authority and technical submittals
- Factory lead: Manufacturing, quality, FAT, and shipment
- Field-service lead: Installation support, SAT, commissioning, and punch-list closure
- Customer project lead: Requirements, approvals, outage coordination, acceptance, and training participation

## Approval gates

Charter approval → design freeze → factory release → FAT acceptance → shipment release → SAT readiness → energization authorization → customer acceptance → financial closeout
